## What this covers

- The video in every cut: the main video, the vertical cut, the C5 section cuts, the animatics and the drafts.
- The music: the Suno v6 track, its stems and MIDI, the mastered track, and the rejected takes.
- The lyrics, captions (`deliverables/*.srt`) and thumbnail.
- Rendered plates, overlay frames and stills under `renders/` and `archive/`.
- The production record, written by the agent: the brief, logs, prompts, style spec, shot list, timing map, and the
  other documents under `docs/`, `log/`, `style/`, `shots/`, `timing/` and `lyrics/`.

## What this doesn't cover

- **Code** (`scripts/`, `scenes/`, `overlays/`, `config/` and the build files): MIT, see [LICENSE](LICENSE). Where an
  overlay file holds both code and on-screen text or design, the code is MIT; the frames it renders are CC BY-NC-SA.
- **Fonts** (`assets/fonts/`): Inter, Instrument Serif and JetBrains Mono, each under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 by
  their authors (the `*-OFL.txt` file beside each font).
- **Reference stills** (`reference/stills/`): frames from other creators' videos, kept as style references. They are
  not ours to license, not covered here and not published; [reference/stills.tsv](reference/stills.tsv) lists the
  source video and time of each.
- **The inspiration videos:** never published; [reference/inspiration.md](reference/inspiration.md) links to them.
